THE PHILOSOPHY

Why Pulse Music Was Built

A rebellion against corporate algorithmic streaming apps that treat listeners like data points and music like background elevator noise.

The Modern Streaming Dilemma

Today's mainstream music apps have lost their soul. They have transformed into noisy advertising engines crowded with video shorts, intrusive subscription walls, algorithmic homogeny, and flat gray-on-gray rectangles that feel more like spreadsheets than hi-fi equipment.

  • Intrusive Surveillance: Continuous telemetry, location logging, and algorithmic profiling.
  • Paywalled Basics: Charging recurring subscriptions for background playback, offline caching, and high-bitrate audio.
  • Sterile Flat Design: Lifeless user interfaces devoid of physical tactility or emotional resonance.
  • Walled Gardens: Restricting playback to proprietary containers and blocking direct file downloads.

The Pulse Music Antidote

Pulse was built to restore reverence to the listening ritual. We combined the tactile, physical nostalgia of vintage high-end studio gear—vacuum-tube amber lighting, needle-dancing VU meters, recessed rotary dials, and beveled cassette decks—with lightning-fast native Kotlin architecture.

  • Zero Tracking & No Accounts: No signups, zero telemetry SDKs, no targeted advertising. Your data never leaves your device.
  • Tactile Skeuomorphism: Authentic hardware decks, LED peak meters, and analog volume pots drawn natively with Jetpack Compose.
  • True Offline Freedom: Dedicated Pure Offline mode with one-tap batch album/playlist downloads and native disk caching.
  • Unified Multi-Source: Stream and download freely across YouTube Music, JioSaavn (320kbps HD), and SoundCloud without walled gardens.
ENGINEERING DECK

Deep Architectural Features

Built from the ground up for stability, audiophile acoustics, and complete listener autonomy.

MOD-01

Multi-Source Stream Router

Aggregates global music catalogs across YouTube Music (via native ytm-kt), JioSaavn (320 kbps high-definition audio), and SoundCloud into a unified crate. Seed-provider affinity ensures that continuous radio sessions stick to your preferred provider with instant failover.

YouTube Music JioSaavn 320k SoundCloud Provider Stickiness
MOD-02

Pure Offline Engine & Queue

Hardware-style toggle cuts all cellular sockets for zero-latency airplane playback. The instant live download queue populates entire albums and playlists immediately with real-time byte-streaming progress indicators and per-task cancellation.

✈️ Flight Mode Live Byte Progress Batch Album DL Room Caching
MOD-03

Pulse JAM Sync Rooms

Real-time multi-listener room broadcasting powered by Firebase Realtime Database. Active timestamp drift auto-correction (<150ms buffer sync) ensures host and guests listen in frame-accurate unison, accompanied by democratic 50% consensus vote-skipping.

Sub-150ms Sync Firebase RTDB Collaborative Queue Vote-Skip
MOD-04

Audiophile Media3 Playback

Engineered on AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer with dedicated foreground MediaSessionService lifecycles. CPU WakeLocks and WifiLocks guarantee that playback never stalls or skips during deep sleep, phone locks, or intensive multitasking.

AndroidX Media3 MediaSession WakeLock Active No Background Stalls
MOD-05

Time-Synced Teleprompter

Full-screen animated lyrics scroller that syncs line-by-line with acoustic timestamps. Warm glowing active lyric badges, fluid kinetic auto-scrolling, and instant distraction-free teleprompter viewing for stage or casual sing-alongs.

Line-by-Line Kinetic Scroll Neumorphic Badges Teleprompter
MOD-06

Zero Telemetry & Local Sovereignty

No accounts, no user telemetry SDKs, no tracking cookies, and no analytical beacon calls. Your listening sessions, curated crates, and cached files live completely on your Android device backed by an encrypted Room SQLite database.

100% Private No Accounts Zero Telemetry Room SQLite
UNDER THE HOOD

Technology Stack & Architecture

Built with Modern Android Development (MAD) standards, clean separation of concerns, and robust automated testing.

LAYER / SUBSYSTEM TECHNOLOGY FUNCTION & PURPOSE
UI & Rendering Jetpack Compose 1.7+ & Material 3 Declarative reactive UI, custom Canvas analog VU meters, LED peak lamps, and skeuomorphic beveled deck components.
Programming Language Kotlin 2.0 Strict null safety, high-performance coroutines, asynchronous flows, and reactive StateFlow state hoisting.
Audio Streaming Core AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer 1.5.0 Audiophile low-latency playback engine, dynamic stream source resolution, and headless MediaSessionService foreground lifecycles.
Local Persistence Room Database (SQLite) + SAF Reactive local caching of tracks, playlists, favorites, offline download tasks, and ID3 metadata storage.
Multi-Source Engines ytm-kt + JioSaavn + SoundCloud Direct stream resolution, dynamic audio link extraction, and fallback failover routing.
Synchronized Rooms Firebase Realtime Database Sub-150ms room state synchronizer, disconnect listener pruning, and democratic live vote-skip consensus.
Continuous Playback CPU WakeLocks & WifiLocks Guaranteed non-stop playback when screen turns off, preventing OS battery-saver killing.
Cloud CI/CD & Delivery GitHub Actions + Static Keystore Automated JVM testing, release APK packaging, cryptographic signature preservation, and automatic OTA updates.
