Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 2026

Your privacy is important to us. It is Pulse Music's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you across our application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We only ask for personal information when we truly need it to provide a service to you. We collect it by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent. Pulse Music operates primarily locally on your device.

  • Local Library Data: Your playlists, favorites, and cached tracks are stored locally on your device using Android Room. We do not transmit this data to our servers.
  • Provider Integration: When you connect to providers like YouTube Music, JioSaavn, SoundCloud, or Deezer, your search queries and streaming requests interact directly with those services. Please refer to their respective privacy policies.

2. Use of Information

We use the collected information for the sole purpose of providing and improving the Pulse Music player experience. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to external sites or integrate with third-party streaming providers that are not operated by us. Please be aware that we have no control over the content and practices of these sites/services, and cannot accept responsibility or liability for their respective privacy policies.

4. Your Consent

By downloading and using Pulse Music, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about how we handle user data and personal information, feel free to contact us through our official GitHub repository.
